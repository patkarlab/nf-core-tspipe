## 2. Decisions (Nikhil)

- Median of exon coverages is the default (D8); mean retained in the summary, not shown.
- 200x is a reportability tier on top of the 100x floor, with a run-level limit (≥ 95% of regions).
- A low gene explained by a consensus LOSS is a finding, not a limitation.
- Known low-capture exons are panel properties: listed with cohort statistics, excluded from the verdict.
- No legacy dashboard link; fastp as numbers with limits, HTML as one link.
